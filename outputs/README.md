# Analysis outputs

Automatically processed candidate water masks, EPSG:4326 GeoJSON polygons, result provenance, and PDF reports are stored under `outputs/analysis/` and excluded from Git.

The generated masks are unvalidated research candidates. They are clipped to a 1.5 km buffer around an OSM reference centerline and must not be interpreted as verified riverbanks, flood detections, erosion/deposition, or scientifically validated change.
