# Data directory

Automatically acquired public Sentinel-1 RTC river-corridor GeoTIFFs, original STAC item metadata, and the local SQLite product catalog are stored here at runtime and excluded from Git. The app reads COG windows from Microsoft Planetary Computer using its read-only SAS token API; only the clipped VV corridor is retained locally.

The product catalog preserves item IDs, acquisition timestamps, footprints, orbit, polarization, source asset URL, license, centerline coverage, processing status, and errors. Signed access tokens are used in memory only and are never saved.

NISAR discovery is automatic through ASF DAAC. Set `EARTHDATA_TOKEN` in the process environment or in the ignored repository-root `.env` file to let the supported `asf_search` client authenticate and download a cataloged GCOV HDF5 product. Downloaded files are size-checked, opened with `h5py`, and registered with their source metadata. The separate GCOV processor reads frequency-A `HHHH`/`HVHV` and the product validity mask in windows, uses projected coordinate axes and CRS, and writes its dual-band measurement GeoTIFF, candidate mask GeoTIFF, display GeoJSON, and processing metadata beside the HDF5 file in `data/acquisitions/<river>/<product>/nisar-processing/` only when paired valid corridor pixels exist. Otherwise, it records no-data metadata and creates no mask or area result. Candidate masks are unvalidated classifications, not verified river boundaries or change results. Frequency B and other NISAR product types are not processed. Without a usable token, the app reports the access restriction and continues with public Sentinel-1 RTC data. The app does not register accounts, bypass access controls, or fabricate acquisitions.

Two Tungabhadra NISAR L2 UR GCOV products acquired on 2026-11-06 are registered locally. Product `NISAR_L2_UR_GCOV_035_012_A_010_4005_DHDH_A_20261106T002554_20261106T002628_P05036_F_F_J_001` has no valid corridor pixels because its acquisition footprint misses the mapped corridor. Product `NISAR_L2_UR_GCOV_035_012_A_009_4005_DHDH_A_20261106T002520_20261106T002555_P05036_F_F_J_001` has valid HH/HV samples and was processed into an unvalidated single-date candidate classification. Its 3,885,882 candidate pixels cover 388.5882 km² within the rasterized corridor; this is not a verified water extent or riverbank measurement. Its GeoJSON display is capped at 5,000 features; use the candidate mask GeoTIFF for the complete classified raster. Kaveri has no downloaded NISAR product in this workflow.

An opt-in component-size sensitivity run generated separate 8-connected GeoTIFF scenarios at minimum sizes 2, 5, and 10 pixels under the A_009 product's `nisar-processing/component-sensitivity/` directory. They reclassify 89,437, 202,003, and 283,567 candidate pixels, respectively, while preserving all 550,634,840 NoData cells and the source grid. The original candidate mask is unchanged. These are exploratory parameter scenarios only; no removed component is established as noise and no cleanup threshold is recommended.

For the A_009 candidate, the processor converts linear HHHH/HVHV power to `10*log10(max(power, 1e-6))` dB and calculates separate Otsu thresholds using 900-bin histograms spanning -60 to +30 dB. HH-only and HV-only comparisons use the same paired-valid pixels; the combined class requires both polarization values to be at or below their separate thresholds. There is no spatial speckle filter or component cleanup. The outputs also include 3-band HH-only/HV-only/combined and 5-band threshold-sensitivity GeoTIFFs, plus 8-connected component counts. The five sensitivity offsets (-2, -1, 0, +1, +2 dB applied equally to both thresholds) are deterministic perturbations, not confidence bounds. The legacy lower/upper area fields shown in the summary represent only the ±1 dB cases.

An independent-reference search found 11 Microsoft Planetary Computer Sentinel-2 L2A catalog items with footprints intersecting portions of the Tungabhadra corridor for 2026-11-05 through 2026-11-08. Bounded actual B03/B08/SCL pixels were read from cloud-optimized assets without saving full tiles. Tile 43PGT produced a separate 10 m EPSG:32643 NDWI candidate mask with 1,011,220 common-valid pixels and 69,498 optical candidates over 6.9498 km². The exploratory mask is available through the API and dashboard; it is not ground truth. Descriptive comparison against the unvalidated NISAR candidate mask gave 74.51% agreement and 20.19% IoU, neither of which is an accuracy or validation score. The partial coverage, ~4 h 55 min acquisition offset, uncalibrated NDWI threshold, SCL classification, and 20 m to 10 m nearest-neighbor resampling preclude treating these data as ground truth. See [SENTINEL2-NISAR-COMPARISON-DIAGNOSTIC.md](./SENTINEL2-NISAR-COMPARISON-DIAGNOSTIC.md). OSM centerlines are context only, not water-boundary ground truth.

Four additional same-overpass 2026-11-06 tiles (43PER, 43PES, 43PFS, 43PFT) were screened and processed into separate exploratory candidate masks. Their common-valid counts are 788,166, 1,630,335, 1,972,759 and 1,266,000 pixels respectively; candidate counts are 28,194, 122,670, 931,867 and 32,019. Supports overlap and must not be summed. Per-item GeoTIFFs and processing metadata are stored under the A_009 product's `nisar-processing/sentinel2-observations/<STAC-item-id>/` directories and are served separately by API/dashboard controls. They do not overwrite 43PGT or serve as ground truth. The metadata-first screen is `backend/scripts/inspect_sentinel2_coverage.py`; bounded candidate generation is `backend/scripts/create_sentinel2_candidate.py`.

An independent-reference investigation screened near-date Landsat C2 scenes and public water datasets for actual spatial/temporal suitability. No ready-made event-date label source or validation scores were found. In a bounded Landsat 9 40×40 window, 955 cells intersected the existing common-valid support and 465 passed QA_PIXEL, band-specific saturation, COG-mask, and nonzero-DN checks. These usable pixels were spatially clustered and did not reveal a defensible channel boundary at 30 m; no reference-label template or labels were created. The scene is 67 h 15 min before NISAR and has 86.63% scene cloud cover. Clearer scenes are approximately 10–12 days earlier. See [INDEPENDENT-VALIDATION-INVESTIGATION.md](./INDEPENDENT-VALIDATION-INVESTIGATION.md) for measured overlap, source/license notes, and the proposed manual-label validation protocol.

Satellite inputs and output analyses may be large. Keep this directory out of Git and back it up separately if needed.
